Privacy Policy

This application is a personal OAuth client for rclone, used solely by its owner to access their own Google Drive.

  • No personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted to the developer or any third party.
  • Files and access tokens remain on the owner's own devices and in the owner's own Google account.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account > Security > Third-party apps.
